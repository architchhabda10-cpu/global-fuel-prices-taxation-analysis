Reading the results responsibly

How to interpret the dashboard

These charts summarize the values present in the supplied dataset. They are descriptive comparisons, not official national estimates or forecasts.

Price and tax patterns can move together without one necessarily causing the other. The dataset does not include every economic, regulatory or supply factor that may explain differences between markets.

Blank or unavailable values are excluded from the corresponding average. Prices are shown in the units stated on each chart.
